# Supericons v2: The Living Map

Recorded 2026-07-06. Status: north-star vision for near-future development. v1 (library, packs, MCP, registry) continues shipping in parallel; v2 is built as concentric rings on top of the same foundations, not as a replacement.

## One sentence

Supericons evolves from an icon library into a living map of programmatic icons, where every icon is an interactive, schema-backed, payment-capable portal to the thing it represents, curated in real time by agents and governed by human taste.

## The primitive: a supericon

A supericon is one entity with five layers:

1. **Face**. The visual: glyph, logo, animation, 3D form. Everything the library sells today.
2. **Soul**. The schema record: identity, purpose, semantics, mind-map keywords, live data, provenance, community feedback. Free tier exposes the core record; the paid tier exposes the design intelligence and extended services data.
3. **Hands**. Services and actions. A supericon does things: play the music inside it, launch the game, swap the token, read the scheduled message, open the storefront, call the MCP tool. The icon stops representing a thing and becomes the portal to the thing.
4. **Pulse**. State and lifecycle. It breathes when idle, swells on events (storm alert, breaking news), appears on schedule (a message timed for a date), grows with earned attention, shrinks when irrelevant, retires when obsolete. The motion vocabulary from the agentic packs (idle, working, attention, done) is the pulse language.
5. **Wallet**. Payments native to both audiences: x402 for agents, card rails for humans. Access, tips, purchases, subscriptions, paid placement.

## The canvas

The site itself becomes the map. No fixed library-grid plus editor columns. Instead:

- Fluid lanes of supericons streaming slowly left and right, rows upon rows, calm by default.
- Hover: the supericon expands slightly and reveals its core info (peek card).
- Click: full card with the complete public record, actions, and the gated extension behind payment.
- Size and position are earned: relevance, events, popularity, and curation move icons; an impending storm swells its alert icon, a quiet brand drifts small.
- The map can re-lens: world map, agent-tools map, a user's personalized map, a trend map.

## The unseen crew

Agents work behind the canvas: adding supericons from events and trends, updating records with the latest info, reorganizing lanes per user preference, executing paid placement instructions. All agent throughput passes a human taste gate before it becomes canonical, the same governance principle as the icons lab: agents propose, humans approve.

## Participation

Users and agents vote, comment, and contribute. Contributions flow through a three-stage pipeline: raw pool (accept everything, with provenance), agent distillation (dedupe, cluster, score against schema slots), human promotion (accepted changes enter the record with contributor credit). Incentives reward acceptance, not submission: promoted contributions earn credits toward gated records, named credit in the record changelog, and agent-submitted feedback earns x402 discounts. Contribution prompts are schema-shaped so quality is manufactured at the source: what did this icon look like at first glance, what did you search before finding it, what did you almost confuse it with.

## The economy and flywheel

- Free public records make every supericon discoverable by humans and agents (SEO plus MCP).
- Paid records sell the design intelligence and service extensions that let anyone, human or frontier agent, adapt and build on a supericon far faster than starting from scratch.
- Supericons with hands transact: music plays, games launch, tokens move, storefronts sell, with supericons taking a toll on access.
- Every interaction emits usage signals; signals become contributions; contributions improve records; better records make better icons and better agent adaptations; better supericons attract more visitors and publishers; more publishers fund more curation.

The flywheel: faces attract traffic, souls make traffic useful to agents, hands monetize, pulses keep people returning, wallets close the loop, and the community sharpens everything each turn.

## Archetypes on the map

Brand logo with live company info. Agent tool with a callable MCP endpoint. Crypto token with live price and swap action. Weather or disaster alert that swells on events. Breaking news flash. A personal message scheduled to reveal on a date for a loved one. A music persona that plays for a micropayment. A launchable mini game. An artist portfolio. A live scoreboard. A custom agent for hire. A whole map inside an icon.

## Build rings (each shippable alone)

1. **Ring 0, now**: two-tier design-record schema (free core, paid intelligence) piloted on 4 pack-2 icons. The schema is written so face, soul, hands, pulse, wallet are native sections; pack 2 icons become the first supericons without rework.
2. **Ring 1**: pack 2 ships (the pulse vocabulary plus revenue). Registry records get the tiered projection.
3. **Ring 2**: feedback pipeline (staging pool, distillation, promotion, credits).
4. **Ring 3**: the canvas: living map UI replacing the grid as the primary browse surface, seeded with the existing 100 plus pack 2.
5. **Ring 4**: hands and wallets: actions on records, x402 access to gated tiers, first service supericons (own catalog first).
6. **Ring 5**: publishers: third parties mint supericons through the taste gate; paid placement; curator agents at scale.

## Principles carried from v1

- Human taste gates agent throughput.
- Records stay honest (draft until reviewed, provenance on every claim).
- Motion has a job and carries rest beats; the map must stay calm.
- Agents are first-class citizens: everything reachable by MCP, payable by x402.
- Design intelligence is the moat: sell the reasoning, not just the artifact.
