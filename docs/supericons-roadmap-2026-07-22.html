Supericons Roadmap

One page connecting the ratified vision to what shipped, what is moving now, and what comes next. Future work is ordered by dependency, never by calendar: each item starts the moment its entry criteria clear.

The vision (charter, ratified 2026-07-17)

Supericons evolves from an icon library into a living map of programmatic icons: every icon an interactive, schema-backed, payment-capable portal to the thing it represents, curated in real time by agents and governed by human taste. Designers, developers, and agents build, share, vote, contribute, earn, and sell. The moat is not secrecy; it is the living ecosystem and its compounding data, which no snapshot can copy.

Jobs to be done

Who Supericons serves and the job each hires it for. Items marked "future" arrive with the milestones below.

Primary customer

AI coding agent

"When my user needs an icon, find the right one in a single call and show it in the chat, so the build never breaks flow."
  • Search by meaning, not just name: brands, concepts, phrases, 12 languages
  • Recommend by task: up to 20 UI slots in one call, clarification when ambiguous
  • Preview inline: rendered contact sheet in the conversation
  • Recover from zero with suggested next queries instead of dead ends
  • Fetch the user's curated sets (future: rung 1)
  • Act and pay programmatically (future: ring 4, x402)
Builder

Developer

"Give me the right icon as usable code in seconds, without accounts, keys, or docs spelunking."
  • Keyless start on every advertised client, copy-paste setup that works
  • Fast local search from the installed package, offline-capable
  • Stable versions with predictable updates and honest changelogs
Builder

Designer

"Help me find, customize, and export icons that match my product's personality."
  • Browse and customize: color, stroke, container, export
  • Motion: animated variants through Motion Lab
  • Expressive originals with personality (growing: si library)
  • Collections that follow them across web and agents (future: rung 1)
Account holder

Customer

Paid user

"Give my money a clear return: premium content, pro tools, and team insight."
  • Premium packs and Pro tools (live: Motion Lab, Converter)

  • Team and fleet analytics, Agentic UI Trends (rung 3)

Contributor and publisher

"Let me add to the map and earn credit and revenue when human taste accepts my work."

  • Publish supericons through the taste gate; sell and earn (ring 5)
  • Curate with agents that propose while humans approve (VC-5)

Standing principles

The rules that outlast any milestone, from the charter and ratified decisions.

Free stays free. The static library and keyless entry are the growth engine, never a concession to claw back.
The moat is living data. Usage-derived intelligence never ships in a public bundle; snapshots age, the service compounds.
Honest beats impressive. Zero results over fabricated relevance; measured claims over adjectives; coverage described as reviewed, not total.
Human taste gates agent scale. Agents propose, humans approve; nothing canonical bypasses judgment.
Agents are first-class citizens. Every capability states its MCP surface; agents pay by x402, humans keep human rails.
Identity is a reward, never a wall. Every rung gives before it asks; keys attribute, they do not gate.
Ship by dependency, not calendar. Everything starts when its entry criteria clear; safety gates never wait, and never rush.
Evidence before architecture. New layers (semantic, model-assisted) exist only when measured demand proves the current layer cannot serve it.
