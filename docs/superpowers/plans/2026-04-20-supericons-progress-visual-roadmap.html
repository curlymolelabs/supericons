What Is Still Not Finished

This keeps the story honest.

Not every icon is covered yet

The first 150-icon set is done, but the full free library is much larger. This is the first proven slice, not the whole finish line.

13 hold records still exist

Those are real edge cases where the wording still needs tightening before approval.

8 reviewed drafts are still intentionally unapproved

These were not forgotten. They were left out on purpose because their meaning is still too broad.

Broader automation is still later

The process is now proven enough to scale, but the large continuous rollout across the wider library is still the next chapter.

Recommended Next Move

This is the highest-value next step from here.

Next recommended step: expand the proven formula to the next icon slice

The strongest next move is not to rethink the first rollout. That part is already proven.

The best next step is to take the same formula that worked here and apply it to the next controlled group of icons, while keeping the hold and reviewed-draft records available for later tightening.
