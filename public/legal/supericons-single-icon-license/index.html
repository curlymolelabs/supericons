What This Covers

This license covers one paid animated icon bought through the Supericons single-icon purchase flow. It includes purchases made by a person, by an agent helping a person, or by an agent approved by an organization.

Who Gets The License

The license belongs to the person or organization that controls the paying wallet or account at the time of purchase. A wallet means the crypto account used to pay.

If an agent buys the icon for someone else, the license belongs to the person or organization that approved the purchase.

What You Can Do

Supericons gives the license holder a worldwide, ongoing license to use the purchased icon in one project, product, website, app, presentation, internal tool, or code repository.

You may:

  • Use the icon in personal, commercial, client, and internal projects.
  • Change the icon for the licensed project.
  • Include the icon in source code or built app files for that project.
  • Let an approved agent place, adapt, or copy the icon into that project.
  • Show the icon in screenshots, demos, documentation, and marketing for that project.

What You Cannot Do

You may not:

  • Resell, sublicense, or share the icon as a standalone asset.
  • Publish the icon in an icon pack, template library, design kit, marketplace item, or asset database.
  • Share the raw SVG or animation CSS as a reusable asset outside the licensed project.
  • Use the icon to build a competing icon library, stock asset product, or training set.
  • Remove receipt or ownership records to hide how the icon was bought.

Agents And Receipts

An agent may buy and use the icon only for the person or organization that approved the purchase. Keep the payment response, settlement reference, or receipt data when available. This helps prove the purchase later.

Refunds are not automatic during the beta. Supericons may review duplicate charges, delivery failures, or mistaken purchases case by case.

Support

For license questions, delivery problems, duplicate charge concerns, or settlement disputes, email hello@supericons.dev with your receipt data and the icon name.
