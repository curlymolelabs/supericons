  Package: `@ip-location-db/geolite2-country-mmdb`
  Upstream: https://github.com/sapics/ip-location-db
  Data source: MaxMind GeoLite2, https://dev.maxmind.com/geoip/geolite2-free-geolocation-data/
  Data license: GeoLite2 End User License Agreement and CC BY-SA 4.0 terms described by MaxMind
