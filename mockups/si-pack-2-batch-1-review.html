Paper-stage review. No shapes are drawn yet, on purpose: approve or redirect each metaphor in words, and only approved ones get geometry. Generated from agent-pulse-batch-1-orbs.json by scripts/build-batch-review.mjs; the records are the source of truth.

The pack law these were authored under (from the agent-pulse pack record)
  • orb present · the agent is involved; no orb means the sound or signal itself
  • wave direction · who acts: waves leaving the orb = emitting, waves arriving = receiving
  • element count · intensity: 1-2 elements quiet, 3 or more loud
  • position semantics · above the orb = needs human attention; sides = communication; corners = burst energy; front overlay = policy
  • diagonal slash · off or muted, and nothing else
  • accent color · Ignition Orange only ever means active or hot; never decoration
  • R1 One idea per icon, at most one secondary element. owner review 2026-07-06, round 1 (busy compositions rejected)
  • R2 Secondary elements draw lighter than the subject (1.8 vs 2 at 24 grid). orb-listening rounds 4-5 (waves lighter than headphones)
  • R3 Sound is wave language (curves), never ray language (straight ticks). sound-blast round 1 (read as sun)
  • R4 Symmetric outward arcs around the orb belong exclusively to speaking; anything else wrapping the orb risks wheel/drone reads. orb-listening round 1 (read as steering wheel)
  • R5 When a side element feels heavy, split it into segments rather than shrinking or doubling it. orb-listening round 5 (split waves)
  • R6 Run the anti-association check before drawing: name what the shape must never read as. rounds 1-2 (sun, wheel, drone all preventable on paper)
  • R7 Keep at least 0.5 optical clearance between distinct elements; 1.5 canvas safety margin. pilot construction recipes
  • R8 Decoration that tells a story the purpose does not need gets cut. sound-blast round 2 (dashed clipping arc removed)
  • R9 Metaphor approval on paper precedes geometry; geometry precedes rendering. pilot workflow, 6 rounds to 1-2 target
