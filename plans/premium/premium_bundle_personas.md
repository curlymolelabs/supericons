# Premium Bundle Buyer Personas

> Phase 1, Step 01 of the Premium Bundle Workflow.
> 10 high-demand icon bundles ranked by market momentum.
> Research date: 2026-03-23

---

## How These Were Selected

Three filters applied to current market trends:

1. **Builder momentum.** What are vibe coders, indie hackers, and solopreneurs actually building right now? (Source: Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, Y Combinator, Reddit r/SaaS)
2. **Icon gap.** Which of those categories have icons that do NOT exist in the 9 free libraries Supericons already offers? Free libraries cover generic UI well. Premium bundles must fill the gaps.
3. **Willingness to pay.** Builders in these categories are already paying $19-49 for UI kits, Figma templates, and boilerplate code. Icons are a natural adjacent purchase.

## Bundle 01: Agentic AI Kit

**Demand rank:** 1 (highest)

**Buyer brief:** Solo developer or small team building an AI-powered product (chatbot, coding assistant, agent orchestrator, RAG pipeline, or LLM wrapper). Uses React/Next.js or Svelte. Cannot design icons. Needs domain-specific concepts like "agent," "prompt," "token," "embedding," "fine-tune," "chain," "tool-use," "context window," and "guardrail" that do not exist in any open-source icon library. Budget: $29 (impulse buy, justified as "saves me 2 hours in Figma").

**Why now:** Vibe coding has exploded since Andrej Karpathy coined the term in early 2025. Every new AI app needs icons for concepts that did not exist 18 months ago. Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code, Devin, and dozens of agent frameworks have created an entirely new visual vocabulary with zero icon coverage.

**Gap analysis:** Free libraries have generic "robot" and "brain" icons. None have: agent workflow, prompt template, token counter, embedding vector, RAG pipeline, model selector, guardrail, context window, fine-tune knob, tool-use connector.

## Bundle 02: SaaS Dashboard Essentials

**Demand rank:** 2

**Buyer brief:** Solopreneur or indie hacker launching a micro-SaaS or vertical SaaS product (client portal, CRM, analytics dashboard, subscription management). Uses Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui or similar component library. Needs a cohesive set of dashboard-specific icons that match their UI kit's aesthetic. Budget: $19.

**Why now:** Micro-SaaS is projected to reach $59.6B by 2030. Lovable, Bolt, and other AI builders have made it trivial to scaffold a SaaS app, but the generated UIs use generic placeholder icons. Builders upgrading from prototype to production need a polished, consistent icon set.

**Gap analysis:** Free libraries cover "settings," "user," "chart." None provide a cohesive set of: onboarding wizard, subscription tier, usage meter, team invite, billing portal, churn indicator, feature flag, A/B test, webhook, audit log.
