# Asset Protection Strategy Note

## Purpose

Capture the current product decision on premium asset protection so we can stop revisiting low-value approaches during launch prep and return later with fresh evidence.

## Current Decision

We will **not** degrade premium previews before launch.

Supericons sells:
- fast discovery
- implementation convenience
- agent and MCP workflow value
- ongoing drops and updates
- account-linked purchases and access

Because of that, preview quality is part of the product itself. If a protection tactic weakens the preview experience, lowers conversion, or makes the animations feel less impressive, it is the wrong tradeoff for now.

## What We Are Optimizing For

1. Strong preview experience that helps conversion.
2. Protected paid workflows where possible.
3. Faster launch and stronger market position.
4. Practical friction against bulk extraction, not fantasy-grade prevention.

## What We Are Not Doing Right Now

The following are explicitly deprioritized unless a new technical breakthrough changes the tradeoff:

- rasterized preview replacement
- video or WebM preview replacement
- low-fidelity placeholder previews
- heavy obfuscation as a primary defense
- “hide the frontend” style anti-clone work

These approaches were explored and did not preserve the real animation experience well enough.

## Working Assumption

Premium previews are marketing.

The real value to protect is not a single visible frame or motion sample. The higher-value surface is:
- bulk extraction
- source-asset convenience
- entitled download/export workflows
- premium MCP access
- account-linked ownership and updates

## Near-Term Posture

Before and at launch:
- keep high-fidelity previews
- keep paid flows and entitlements server-backed
- avoid shipping changes that make previews worse
- compete on workflow, speed, quality, updates, and ecosystem fit

## Later Revisit Areas

When launch pressure is lower, revisit asset protection through higher-leverage directions:

- request throttling and scraping detection
- traceable or fingerprinted premium exports
- entitlement-aware API delivery patterns
- bulk-access controls
- abuse monitoring and operational response

## Revisit Trigger

Re-open this topic only if one of these becomes true:

- scraping or cloning causes measurable revenue loss
- premium bundle exposure becomes a clear operational problem
- a new technique preserves full preview quality while reducing extraction usefulness
- Supericons expands into a broader asset-delivery or licensing platform

## Product Opportunity Note

There may be a future standalone opportunity around agent-safe asset delivery, traceable creative exports, or entitlement-aware media APIs. That is a separate product exploration, not a launch blocker for Supericons today.
