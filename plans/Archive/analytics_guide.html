How to Read Your Stats

Steps

  1. Go to cloud.umami.is and log in
  2. Click Supericons in the website list
  3. Top bar: visitors, views, bounce rate, avg time
  4. Click Events tab for custom events
  5. Click any event to expand data fields

Trending Leaderboard (Feature Flag)

The sidebar has a hidden "Trending" section showing top 10 most-used icons. Controlled by a feature flag in main.js:

const FEATURE_FLAGS = {
  leaderboard: false, // change to true to show
};

Data is captured from day 1 regardless of the flag. Flip to true, rebuild, and redeploy when ready.
