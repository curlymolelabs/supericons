## Brand Synopsis

Supericons is a free, open-source icon search engine and customization platform built for modern UI/UX designers and frontend developers. Rather than being a single icon library, it unifies 19,000+ icons from 9 of the best open-source libraries (Material Symbols, Lucide, Tabler, Phosphor, Heroicons, Bootstrap, Ionicons, Iconoir, and Simple Icons) into one searchable, customizable workspace. The core problem it solves: developers waste 5 to 15 minutes per icon going from browse to production. Supericons cuts that to 5 seconds.

Powered by AI semantic search (220+ synonym groups, 5-layer matching), true Material Symbols variable font axes, and one-click component export (React, Vue, Svelte, HTML), Supericons gives creators the "superpower" to find, customize, and ship icons faster than any tool on the market. The brand stands for speed, precision, and zero friction, distilling the fragmented icon ecosystem into a single, instant workflow.

### Logo Identity

Ultra-minimalist, flat SVG geometry. No gradients, no complex shading, no literal cliches. A single, memorable silhouette that balances the "Super" aspect (speed, power, aggregation) with the digital nature of "Icons" (clean geometry, pixel precision). The mark should feel at home as a favicon, an app icon, and a watermark on exported ZIP files.

### Typography

- **Typeface:** Nunito Black 900 Italic
- **Tracking:** -0.02em (tight, cohesive, modern tech feel)
- **Size:** 1.375rem for header wordmark
- **Rendering:** Primary brand color against dark (#111) or warm light (#faf8f4) backgrounds

### Color Palette

- **Primary:** Supericons Orange (#FF4F00)
- **Application:** Solid color blocks only. No gradients on the brand mark. High-energy orange stands out against two supported themes: deep charcoal dark mode (#111111) and warm linen light mode (#faf8f4). Also used for active navigation states, link highlights, and primary CTA accents throughout the application.

## Socratic Prioritization

### Q1. What does the user open the app to do?
**Search -> Customize -> Export.** Export is where value is delivered.

### Q2. Where does the current experience break?
| Step | Friction | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Search | Synonym search works (220+ groups). 19,608 icons. Popularity sort live. | Low |
| Browse | 9 libraries. Outline/solid toggle. Ionicons stroke scaling fixed. | Low |
| Export | React/Vue/Svelte/HTML done. PNG/SVG/ICO/Base64/ZIP done. Angular/Flutter deferred. | Low |

### Q3. Verdict
**Library expansion is the highest-impact next build.** More icons = more reasons to visit, more search results, bigger data moat.
